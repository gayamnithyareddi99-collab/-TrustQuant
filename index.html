How It Works

From Self-Attested Questionnaires to Verified Continuous Trust Intelligence

Phase 01 · Year 1

UK Soft Launch & Product Validation

A London and South-East focused pilot programme trains the first UK-specific machine learning models on SME cloud configurations, Linux system telemetry, identity access profiles, and administrative privilege logs consistent with NCSC Cyber Essentials v3.3 guidelines.

Co-founders Chaitanya Nagabhairu and Laxman Rao Yennamaneni lead paid pilots at 5–10 UK SME locations, integrating agentless API connectors and Linux inspection scripts to build the first Digital Resilience Fingerprints and validate the CLRTL risk translation logic.

Phase 02 · Year 2

Full UK Launch & Model Refinement

The TrustQuant Resilience Rating scales to 50–100 SME client nodes across Manchester, Birmingham, and South-West tech hubs, with plug-and-play API connectivity to UK commercial lending portals, credit scoring bureaus, and major MSP systems (ConnectWise, Autotask).

SME Configuration Drift Autocalibration (CDAP) models self-calibrate their rating outputs based on real UK SME drift behaviours, and the Trust Passport generation becomes fully automated, reducing compliance processing from months to minutes.

Phase 03 · Year 3+

Scale, Network Effects & International Expansion

The Community Cyber-Intelligence Registry (CCIR) goes live with federated machine learning, sharing anonymised UK risk trends across SMEs by industry sector, region, and company size creating powerful network effects no competitor can easily replicate.

The platform expands to North England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland, then launches early EU pilots in Germany, France, and the Nordic tech hubs, aligning with the EU NIS2 Directive scaling toward 500+ enterprise nodes globally through cloud-native, modular SaaS architecture.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is the cyber-to-financial trust gap?

+
It's the gap between a business's technical security posture and its ability to prove digital trustworthiness to lenders, insurers, and enterprise buyers. Over 78% of UK SMEs fall into the "low" or "very low" categories of cyber resilience, yet the vast majority rely on static, easily falsified self-attestation questionnaires that provide no real-time financial risk visibility. TrustQuant bridges this gap with verified, continuously refreshed evidence.

Do I need to install agent software on my servers?

+
No. TrustQuant is fully agentless. Our Agentless Metadata Extraction Protocol (AMEP) uses secure, read-only REST API connectors to extract infrastructure metadata from AWS, Azure, and GCP environments without requiring any performance-heavy agent software to be installed on your hosts. Secure cloud connection is achievable in under 10 minutes.

How is this different from BitSight or SecurityScorecard?

+
BitSight and SecurityScorecard rely on passive external scanning of public IP addresses and DNS records they are completely blind to an SME's internal cloud configurations, Linux server hardening states, and credential access patterns. Neither translates technical posture into credit risk parameters that commercial lenders require. TrustQuant is the only platform that bridges internal infrastructure evidence directly to financial underwriting intelligence, at SME-affordable pricing.

What results have pilot SMEs seen?

+
An 8-week pilot across 5 UK SME locations showed a 25% average reduction in high-risk technical vulnerabilities, with cloud-heavy startups achieving 40% risk reduction funded directly by identified cloud-cost savings. User satisfaction was 4.3/5 for underwriting and lending use cases, and operators reported immediate improvements in cloud-cost efficiency, backup restore confidence, and supply-chain reporting.

Is TrustQuant compliant with UK data protection regulations?

+
Yes. TrustQuant is built around strict "privacy-by-design" principles, extracting read-only technical metadata and never writing or modifying system data. The platform is natively aligned with UK GDPR (Data Protection Act 2018), the NCSC's Cyber Essentials Plus v3.3 guidelines, the UK Cyber Governance Code of Practice, and the upcoming UK Cyber Security and Resilience Bill with all client telemetry encrypted using a hybrid TwoFish/RSA cryptographic model.

How does the Trust Passport help secure better financing?

+
The SME Trust Passport is a cryptographically verifiable digital asset that consolidates all verified infrastructure evidence and financial risk translation into stakeholder-specific reports. SMEs can instantly share this with commercial lenders to demonstrate repayment capacity, with cyber-insurers to secure lower premiums (up to 25% reduction), and with enterprise procurement teams to clear supplier onboarding audits turning digital resilience into a direct commercial competitive advantage.
